# Locked Architectural Decisions

Decisions that future work must preserve unless explicitly reopened with the
project owner. The point of writing these down is that a fresh session can't
know them, and will otherwise "improve" them away.

---

### <Decision>

**Decided** — what was decided.

**Why** — the reasoning at the time.

**Affects** — which modules depend on this holding.

**If reversed** — what breaks, and what the migration cost would be.

**Status** — locked / open to revisit when _(condition)_
