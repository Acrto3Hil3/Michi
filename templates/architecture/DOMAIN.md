# Domain Model

One section per business domain. Add domains as they're built — don't stub out
domains that don't exist yet.

---

## <Domain name>

**Responsibility** — what this domain is accountable for, in one line.

**Owns** — the tables/entities that belong to it.

**Key rules** — the business rules that must always hold here.

**Depends on** — other domains it reads from, and why.

**Depended on by** — who breaks if this changes.

**Permissions** — which roles can do what.
